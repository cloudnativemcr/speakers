# Cloud Native Manchester 🐝

We are an active [Cloud Native Computing Foundation](https://www.cncf.io) regional community group for Manchester, UK.

## Meetups

Please join the [CNCF Manchester Chapter](https://community.cncf.io/cloud-native-manchester/) on the [CNCF Community website](https://community.cncf.io). New events are published on our CNCF chapter page. Please RSVP on the relevant event page if you’d like to attend.

> If you are currently a member of our [Meetup.com group](https://www.meetup.com/cloud-native-kubernetes-manchester/), please join the Cloud Native Manchester group on the [CNCF Community website](https://community.cncf.io/cloud-native-manchester/) instead.

### What to Expect

If you're joining us for the first time, here's our typical format: doors open at 6 pm, and talks start at 7 pm. We usually host two talks, with a break in between. We also host lightning talks for anyone who wishes to give a short presentation.

## Speaker Submissions

We currently support speaker submissions via Sessionize or email.

### Via Sessionize

Please see the Cloud Native Manchester Call for Speakers on [the Sessionize website](https://sessionize.com/manchester-cloud-native-meetup/), where you can submit details of the talk you wish to present.

### Via Email

Alternatively, you can submit your talk by emailing [contact@cloudnativemcr.co.uk](mailto:contact@cloudnativemcr.co.uk), and one of the organisers will get back to you.

When doing so, please ensure you include the following details:

```
* **Title**:
* **Speaker**:
* **Type**: (Presentation: 30–45 minutes | Lightning talk: 5–10 minutes)
* **Level**: (basic | standard | advanced)
* **Tags**: (e.g. Kubernetes, DevOps, live coding, workshop)

**Description**

*Please provide a short paragraph that describes your proposal.*

**Speaker Bio** (optional)

*Please provide a short bio of the speaker.*
```

## Code of Conduct

As a CNCF community group, all attendees, speakers, sponsors, and volunteers are required to follow the [CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md). We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone. If you experience or witness any unacceptable behaviour, please contact the [organisers](mailto:contact@cloudnativemcr.co.uk).
